The toolkit

How to use each tool

Six focused tools, each simple on its own and built to work together. Pick one to see how it works.

Notes

Capture ideas the moment they arrive. Notes keeps every thought in one place, saved on your device so it is there when you come back.

  1. Open a new note from the add button.
  2. Give it a title, write your content and save it.
  3. Reopen any note to edit it or delete it, and use the filter to narrow your list.

Your notes stay on this device, even after you close the tab.

Open Notes

Todo

Turn a busy day into a short, clear list. Todo helps you add tasks, set priorities and watch your progress grow.

  1. Type a task into “Add a new task” and add it.
  2. Set its priority and reorder tasks so the important ones come first.
  3. Tick tasks off to track progress, and switch between all and completed.

Finish everything on your list and Workspaces celebrates with you.

Open Todo

Stopwatch

Three time tools in one place: a stopwatch for tracking effort, a countdown timer for focused sessions and alarms to keep you on schedule.

  1. Choose Stopwatch, Timer or Alarm from the tabs.
  2. Start, pause and reset the stopwatch, or set a countdown and let it run.
  3. Add an alarm with a label so you always know what it is for.

Timers and alarms ring while the Workspaces tab stays open, so keep it open in the background.

Open Stopwatch

Calendar

See your month at a glance and plan with purpose. Create events with a time and notes, and keep your next commitments in view.

  1. Select a day or the add button to create an event with a title, date and start time.
  2. Search your events, or use “Go to date” to jump anywhere in the calendar.
  3. Check “Next events” to see what is coming up.

Events can be exported as an .ics file to use in other calendar apps.

Open Calendar

Calculator

More than basic maths: a standard and a scientific calculator, percentage tools, unit and currency converters, and a history of your calculations.

  1. Choose Standard or Scientific mode.
  2. Use Percentage Tools, the Unit Converter or the Currency Converter for everyday conversions.
  3. Look back at past results in Calculation History.

For currencies, enter a custom rate to use today’s exact exchange rate.

Open Calculator

Links

Keep the sites you use every day one tap away. Save links with a name and a category, then search or filter to find them fast.

  1. Select “Add New Link” and enter a name, a URL and a category.
  2. Save it and it appears in “Your Links”.
  3. Search or filter by category to jump straight to what you need.

Include the full address starting with https:// so the link opens correctly.

Open Links
The story

The story of Workspaces

Work is leaving the office. Teams are spread across cities and time zones, yet the tools they rely on are scattered across a dozen tabs. Workspaces starts from a simple belief: a workspace should be somewhere you can open from anywhere, not a building you have to travel to.

That future will not arrive in one release, so it is being built in stages. Today, Workspaces is a fast personal hub that gets the fundamentals right: notes, tasks, time, plans and links. Each tool is deliberately small and focused, because the habits people build alone are the habits teams will later share.

  1. Live

    Stage 1: Your personal workspace

    Notes, Todo, Stopwatch and timers, Calendar, Calculator and Links, working offline and saved on your own device.

  2. Next

    Stage 2: Accounts and sync

    Sign in and pick up your workspace on any device, with secure cloud backup.

  3. Planned

    Stage 3: Teams

    Shared workspaces where people can assign tasks, share calendars and work on notes together.

  4. Planned

    Stage 4: Admin management

    Roles and permissions, progress dashboards and reports, so admins can guide the work without micromanaging it.

  5. Vision

    Stage 5: Remote-first

    Presence, time zones, time tracking and async updates, so teams work together with no physical location at all.

Roadmap stages are plans, and timing may change as Workspaces grows.
