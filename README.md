\# Workspaces 🚀\
\
\*\*Your productivity. Your space. Everything in one place.\*\*\
\
Workspaces is a modern, responsive productivity web application designed to bring essential everyday tools together in one organized and customizable environment.\
\
Whether you're managing tasks, writing notes, tracking time, or organizing useful links, Workspaces gives you the tools to stay focused and productive.\
\
\## ✨ Features\
\
\- \*\*Notes\*\* – Create, edit, delete, and automatically save your notes.\
\- \*\*Todo Manager\*\* – Organize and track your daily tasks.\
\- \*\*Calculator\*\* – Perform everyday calculations.\
\- \*\*Calendar\*\* – View dates and organize your schedule.\
\- \*\*Stopwatch\*\* – Track time with a built-in stopwatch.\
\- \*\*Quick Links\*\* – Save and organize frequently visited websites.\
\- \*\*Clock & Date\*\* – Keep track of the current time and date.\
\- \*\*Daily Quotes\*\* – Get a little inspiration throughout your day.\
\- \*\*Dark & Light Mode\*\* – Switch between themes for a comfortable experience.\
\- \*\*Progressive Web App (PWA)\*\* – Install Workspaces on supported devices and access supported features offline.\
\
\## 🛠️ Built With\
\
\- HTML5\
\- CSS3\
\- JavaScript\
\- Font Awesome\
\- Progressive Web App (PWA) technologies\
\- Local Storage\
\
\## 📱 Responsive Design\
\
Workspaces is designed to provide a consistent experience across desktop, tablet, and mobile devices.\
